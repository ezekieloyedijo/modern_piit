FAQ

Common certificate verification questions answered

Result not showing

If your certificate does not appear, please check the spelling and try again. Use the full student name or contact us for manual lookup if the issue persists.

Wrong name on certificate

If a name is incorrect, submit a certificate correction request. We verify your details and issue a corrected certificate promptly.

Download not working

If downloading fails, refresh the page or try a different browser. Contact support with the search result details so we can resend the download link.

Verification failed

Verification may fail if details do not match our records. Confirm the exact name and contact student affairs for verification assistance.

How employers can verify certificates

Employers can use the certificate search service, check the QR code on printed certificates, or contact our registrar to confirm authenticity.

Processing time for requests

Most certificate requests are processed within 1-3 business days. Correction and support ticket requests may take slightly longer depending on verification.

Verification Trust

Trusted certificate verification for employers and partners

How employers verify certificates

Employers can verify certificates by searching our records, reviewing certificate details, and validating the issued credential with our registrar team.

QR verification explanation

Each official certificate includes a QR code that links back to our verification system, ensuring instant validation and anti-counterfeit protection.

Official disclaimer

All certificates issued by PIIT are authentic, traceable, and backed by our academic registry for full employer confidence.
