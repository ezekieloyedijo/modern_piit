Trainings

  • Smart Attendance System

    A Smart Attendance System is an intelligent, technology-driven solution designed to automate the process of recording, tracking, and managing attendance of individuals

  • Smart Waste Sorter

    A Smart Waste Sorter is a modern system that uses technologies like Artificial Intelligence (AI) and sensors to automatically detect and separate different types of waste, such as plastic, metal, glass, paper, and food waste, into their correct categories

  • AI Story Generator

    An AI Story Generator is a computer program that uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) to create stories automatically based on the words, ideas, or themes given by a user. 

  • AI Plant Identifier

    An AI Plant Identifier is a smart application that uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) to recognize and identify plants from photos or live camera input. It analyzes the plant’s leaves, flowers, or overall shape to determine its name, species, and other details.
