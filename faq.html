Is your certificate recognized nationwide?

Yes.

Can I put my classes on hold in case of emergency or take the virtual option for classes?

Yes. You’ll need to inform the counsellor and your faculty for approval.

What is NBTE?

National Board for Technical Education.

Do I get to complete all my classes with one tutor?

No. We have various faculties for different modules, courses and semesters.

Do you offer job placements or internships?

Yes.

Will I get something if I refer someone?

Yes, we give referral commission.

What are the criteria's to getting my certificate of completion?

You will be required to carry out a project that you will also present/defend at the end of your training period.

When are certificates issued?

Certificates are issued after the completion course work and projects.

JAMB CBT Centre: what should I bring on exam day?

Bring your JAMB exam slip and arrive early. Always follow the instructions printed on your JAMB slip, as JAMB sets the exact requirements for each exam. The centre is preparing to open: see the CBT Centre page for updates.

JAMB CBT Centre: how do I find the centre?

The PIIT JAMB CBT Centre is at Amazing Grace Mall, Lakowe, Lekki Epe Road, Lagos, with easy access from Lekki, Ajah, Sangotedo, Awoyaya and Ibeju Lekki. There is a map on the CBT Centre page.

JAMB CBT Centre: who do I contact?

Call 08105879530, chat with us on WhatsApp (07070069959), or email enquiries@piit.com.ng.
