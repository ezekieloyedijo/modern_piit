Our Story

A Legacy of Innovation in
Tech Education

Prime Innovation Institute of Technology (PIIT) started its journey in 2009 as Prime Value County, driven by a mission to bridge the gap between education and technology. A year later, in 2010, we rebranded as Prime Innovation and earned accreditation from the National Board for Technical Education (NBTE). PIIT began as a JAMB centre and grew into a specialist IT institute focused on software engineering.

Today PIIT trains students at its HFP centre on Abraham Adesanya, Lekki, Ajah, Lagos, through its NBTE accredited Diploma in Software Engineering, and is preparing to open a JAMB CBT Centre at Lakowe. Over 22,000 students have been trained by PIIT in Lagos.
