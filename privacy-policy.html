This Privacy Policy explains how Prime Innovation Institute of Technology (“PIIT”, “we”, “us” or “our”), RC 960999, collects, uses, shares and protects personal information. It covers our website at piit.com.ng and our pages, messages and advertisements on Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp. It also covers any other way you contact us, such as phone, email or visiting our centre.

We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the rules of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who we are

PIIT is an NBTE-accredited institute in Lagos, Nigeria, affiliated with Bellarks Polytechnic, Kwale, Delta State. We offer the Diploma in Software Engineering, kids coding, exam tutorials and a JAMB CBT Centre. PIIT is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy.

Address: Block E1, Suite 061/084 and Block D2, Suite 306/307, HFP EastLine Shopping Complex, Abraham Adesanya, Lekki, Ajah, Lagos, Nigeria.
Email: enquiries@piit.com.ng  ·  Phone: 08105879530

2. Information we collect

Information you give us
  • Contact and enquiry forms: your name, email address, phone number and the message you send.
  • LSETF training applications: full name, email address, phone number, age range, Local Government Area, LASRRA registration number, highest qualification, employment status and preferred training track.
  • Partner and internship requests from organisations: company name, contact person, email, phone number, industry, and the internship details you provide.
  • Admission and enrolment: when you register for a programme, we collect the details needed to admit and teach you. These include identification details, a passport photograph, academic records, payment records and, for students under 18, parent or guardian details.
  • Messages: the content of messages you send us on WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, email or phone, together with your name, profile name and phone number as shown on that platform.
  • Lead forms on Facebook and Instagram: if you fill in a form in one of our ads, we receive the details you choose to submit, usually your name, phone number, email address and the programme you are interested in.
Information collected automatically
  • Technical data: when you visit our website, our hosting server automatically records standard log information. This includes your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and the date and time of your visit.
  • Certificate searches: when you use our certificate verification page, we process the name or certificate number you search for to return matching results.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data, such as health, religious or biometric information, through our website.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and messages.
  • To process applications, admissions and enrolment, and to deliver our training, tutorials and CBT services.
  • To check eligibility for sponsored programmes such as the LSETF training, and to share required information with the sponsoring body.
  • To connect our students with organisations offering internships.
  • To issue and verify certificates.
  • To manage fees, payments and student records.
  • To send you information about our programmes, events and intakes. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To keep our website secure, prevent misuse, and improve our services.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory and accreditation duties.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on one or more of these grounds under the NDPA:

  • Consent: for example, when you submit a form or agree to receive updates. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contract: to admit, teach and certify you once you enrol.
  • Legal obligation: to meet requirements of regulators, accreditation bodies and the law.
  • Legitimate interests: to run, secure and improve our services, where this does not override your rights.

5. Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp

We use Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp, which are services of Meta Platforms, Inc. and its affiliates (“Meta”), to share updates, advertise our programmes and talk with prospective and current students.

  • When you message us, comment on our posts, or submit a lead form in one of our ads, we receive the information you share and the public profile details Meta makes available to us.
  • We use this information only to reply to you, process your enquiry or application, and tell you about our programmes.
  • We do not sell information we receive from Meta, and we do not use it for any purpose that Meta’s Platform Terms do not allow.
  • Meta processes your information under its own terms. Please read the Meta Privacy Policy and the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.

To have us delete information we received through Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp, follow the steps in section 11.

6. Who we share information with

We share personal information only when necessary, and only with:

  • Programme partners and sponsors: Bellarks Polytechnic (our affiliate institution), the Lagos State Employment Trust Fund (LSETF) for applicants to LSETF-sponsored training, and examination bodies such as JAMB where you use our CBT services.
  • Internship partners: organisations offering internships, when you are being considered for a placement.
  • Service providers who help us run our services, under confidentiality obligations. These include our website host, Formspree (which delivers our website form submissions to us), email providers, and Meta for messaging and advertising.
  • Authorities: regulators, law enforcement or courts, where the law requires it or to protect our rights, students or staff.

Certificate verification: to let employers and institutions confirm our certificates, our certificate verification page shows the holder’s name and certificate details to anyone who searches for them. If you hold a PIIT certificate and want it removed from the search, contact us.

7. Cookies and third-party content

Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies of its own. Some pages load content from other services, which may set their own cookies or collect your IP address:

  • Embedded YouTube videos (Google).
  • Fonts from Google Fonts and icons from public content networks (cdnjs and jsDelivr).
  • Links that open WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn or Google Maps.

These services handle your data under their own privacy policies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If we add analytics or advertising tools, such as the Meta Pixel, in the future, we will update this policy first.

8. How long we keep your information

  • Enquiries and leads that do not lead to enrolment: up to 24 months after our last contact with you.
  • Applications that are not successful: up to 12 months after the end of the intake.
  • Student records: for as long as needed to provide our services, and afterwards for as long as required by law and accreditation rules.
  • Certificate records: kept long-term, so that certificates can always be verified.

When we no longer need your information, we delete it or make it anonymous.

9. How we protect your information

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data. These include encrypted (HTTPS) connections, password-protected systems, and limiting access to staff who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify you and the NDPC as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Under the NDPA, you have the right to:

  • be told how your personal data is used (this policy);
  • get a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to how we use your data, including for marketing;
  • withdraw your consent at any time;
  • receive your data in a commonly used format and have it sent to another organisation; and
  • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng) if you are unhappy with how we handle your data.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 15. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We will respond within 30 days.

11. How to request deletion of your data

You can ask us to delete the personal data we hold about you at any time. This includes data we received through our website, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp or lead forms.

  1. Email enquiries@piit.com.ng with the subject line “Data Deletion Request”. You can also send the same request on WhatsApp to 07070069959.
  2. Include your full name, and the phone number, email address or social media profile you used to contact us, so we can find your records.
  3. We will confirm when we receive your request. We will then delete your data within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

We may need to keep some records where the law requires it, for example student, payment or certificate records. If so, we will tell you what we are keeping and why.

To remove PIIT’s access to your Facebook information, go to your Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites and remove PIIT. This stops future sharing. To delete data we already hold, send us a request as described above.

12. Children’s privacy

Some of our programmes, such as kids coding and exam tutorials, are for people under 18. We collect personal data about a child only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. A parent or guardian can view, correct or delete their child’s data at any time by contacting us. Children should not submit forms or send us personal information without a parent’s or guardian’s permission. If we learn that we have collected a child’s data without this consent, we will delete it.

13. International transfers

Some of our service providers, including Meta, Google and Formspree, may store or process data outside Nigeria. Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards allowed under the NDPA, such as the provider’s data protection commitments, to keep your information protected.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. If the changes are significant, we will make that clear on our website or contact you directly.
